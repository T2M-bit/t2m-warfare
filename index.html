02

MEMBERSHIP

  • ▸ Stay active in the T2M community.
  • ▸ Competitive players must maintain regular activity & commitment.
  • ▸ Respect all members — no toxicity, racism, harassment or discrimination.
03

COMMUNICATION

  • ▸ Follow instructions from Leaders/Co-Leaders.
  • ▸ Discord is T2M's official communication platform.
  • ▸ Check Discord regularly and respond to important mentions.
  • ▸ No spam, provocation or unnecessary conflicts.
04

PARTICIPATION

  • ▸ Registered/assigned matches are mandatory unless excused.
  • ▸ Complete assigned objectives, missions or minimum points.
  • ▸ Inform Management early if unable to participate.
05

MEMBER TIDAK AKTIF

  • ▸ Inactive on Discord for an extended period will receive a warning first.
  • ▸ Members will be given a chance to return and communicate with T2M.
  • ▸ Inform Management if you need to be inactive.
  • ▸ No improvement after the given opportunity → Management may remove/kick the member.
Being active in-game does not replace Discord activity or communication.
06

MATCH RULES

  • ▸ Follow plans, strategies and assigned roles.
  • ▸ Do not act randomly when coordination is required.
  • ▸ Opt out early if unable to participate.
  • ▸ Team coordination comes first.
07

FAIR PLAY

  • ▸ No hacking, cheating, exploits or unauthorized third-party tools.
  • ▸ No leaking T2M strategies, screenshots or internal information.
  • ▸ Maintain sportsmanship and respect.
08

SAFETY & CONDUCT

T2M welcomes members of all ages, including minors.
  • ▸ Appropriate language and behaviour are REQUIRED.
  • ▸ No harassment, bullying, threats, sexual/inappropriate content or behaviour that makes others uncomfortable.
  • ▸ Respect personal boundaries. Do not repeatedly DM or disturb members without consent.
Serious violations may result in immediate removal.
